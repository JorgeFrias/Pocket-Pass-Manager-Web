Support

Email us at contact@reliby.com

We read every message and usually reply within a few working days. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and app version. Never send us your passwords or master password: we will never ask for them.

Common questions

I forgot my master password. Your vault exists only on your iPhone and we have no copy, so we cannot reset or recover it.

Moving to a new iPhone. Create an encrypted backup in the app, save it to iCloud Drive or another place you can reach, and restore it on the new iPhone.

Purchases and refunds. Purchases go through the App Store. To restore a purchase on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data collected? No. See the Privacy Policy.
