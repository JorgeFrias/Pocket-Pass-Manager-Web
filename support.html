Frequently asked questions

Does Pocket Pass Manager work offline?

Yes. The app never connects to the internet. Your vault is stored and encrypted on your iPhone, and AutoFill, the authenticator and the security report all work without the internet.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, in a database encrypted with AES-256. There is no account and no cloud sync, and we have no server that holds your data.

How do I back up my vault?

Create an encrypted backup in the app and save it wherever you like, for example in iCloud Drive. To restore it you need both the master password you had when you made it and your recovery code. Find the recovery code in Backup Settings and keep a copy somewhere safe, away from your iPhone.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Create an encrypted backup on your old iPhone, save it somewhere you can reach from the new one, such as iCloud Drive, and restore it on the new iPhone with your master password and your recovery code. Save the recovery code from Backup Settings before you leave the old iPhone.

I forgot my master password. Can you reset it?

No. Your vault exists only on your iPhone and we have no copy of it or of your password, so we cannot reset or recover it.

How much does it cost?

Pocket Pass Manager is free for up to 20 passwords, 10 authenticator codes and 5 notes. A one-time purchase removes the limits. There is no subscription.

How do I restore a purchase or get a refund?

Purchases go through the App Store. To restore a purchase on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data collected?

No. The app has no analytics and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
