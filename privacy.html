Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: the Pocket Pass Manager app collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no server of ours. Everything you store stays encrypted on your iPhone, and nothing leaves it unless you send it yourself.

Who we are

Pocket Pass Manager is made by Reliby Solutions S.L., a company registered in Spain ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, write to contact@reliby.com.

This policy covers the Pocket Pass Manager iPhone app, its AutoFill extension and widget, and this website.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking software. It does not connect to any server run by us or by anyone else on our behalf. On the App Store, its privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

You can check this yourself with the App Privacy Report in iOS Settings, which lists every network connection an app makes.

Your vault

Passwords, two-factor codes, cards, Wi-Fi networks, HomeKit codes and notes are stored in a vault on your iPhone, encrypted with AES-256 and protected by your master password. We never receive your vault or your master password, and we cannot recover them for you.

Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded; it never receives your face or fingerprint data.

Features that use your device

AutoFill. When you fill a login or a verification code in Safari or another app, the AutoFill extension reads it from your vault on the device. Nothing is sent to us.

Widget and StandBy. The widget shows the codes you choose on your Home Screen or in StandBy. They are generated on your iPhone.

Two-factor codes. Codes are calculated on your iPhone from the secrets you add. No connection is needed.

Camera. Used only to scan QR codes, such as a two-factor setup code, a Wi-Fi code or a contact card for sharing. Images are processed on the device and not kept.

Location. Only if you choose to pin a Wi-Fi network on a map. The place you pick is saved with that network inside your encrypted vault. The map itself is provided by Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy.

Security report. Checks for common and reused passwords run on your iPhone, against a list built into the app.

When data leaves your iPhone

Only when you decide it should:

Backups. You can create an encrypted backup file and save it wherever you like, for example in your own iCloud Drive. If you save it to iCloud Drive, it is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's terms; we have no access to it. The backup stays encrypted.

Sharing a password. When you share a login with a trusted contact, the app encrypts it end to end for that person, and you send the file with the app of your choice (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files and so on). Only the recipient's copy of Pocket Pass Manager can open it. No account and no server of ours are involved.

Using your vault from a computer. When you turn on the local server, your iPhone serves a page to browsers on the same local network, and the app encrypts the vault data it sends. It does not use the internet, and the data goes only to the computer you connect from.

Purchases

The one-time purchase that removes the free limits is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks your purchase receipt on the device. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

App Store reviews and statistics

The app may ask you to rate it using Apple's standard prompt. Apple may give us aggregated App Store statistics, such as downloads and, if you opted in to share them with developers, anonymous crash and usage figures. These do not identify you.

This website

The home page of pocketpassmanager.com uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google may set cookies and process your IP address and browser details for this, under Google's privacy policy. We use this data only in aggregate. You can block it with your browser's tracking protection or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The Privacy, Terms and Support pages do not use analytics.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights (GDPR)

The app holds no personal data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete: your data is on your iPhone, under your control. Deleting the app deletes the vault from the device.

For website analytics and emails, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Write to contact@reliby.com. The legal basis for analytics is our legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used, and for emails it is answering your request. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, or to the authority where you live.

Children

Pocket Pass Manager is not directed at children, and because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top. If a change ever meant collecting data, we would say so clearly in the app before it happened.
