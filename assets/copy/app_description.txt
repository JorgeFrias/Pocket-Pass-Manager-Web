### New in Pocket Pass Manager 3.0

- **Redesigned for iOS 27**, with native sheets everywhere. Tap any field to copy it.
- **Search your whole vault** from its own tab: passwords, codes, cards, Wi-Fi, notes and HomeKit.
- **Share passwords with people you trust**, end-to-end encrypted. No account and no cloud.
- **Verification codes in AutoFill**, right above the keyboard.
- **A widget for your Home Screen and StandBy**, with your most used codes.
- **Safer by design:** when the app locks, nothing stays decrypted, and secrets are read only when you use them.

- **Security report:** an offline check finds common and reused passwords, so you know what to change first.

**Passwords and AutoFill.** Save logins and fill them in Safari and other apps. Create strong passwords or easy-to-type passphrases with the built-in generator.

**Built-in authenticator.** Generate two-factor (2FA) codes, TOTP and HOTP, without a separate authenticator app. Scan a QR code to add one and link it to its login.

**Everything else worth keeping safe.** Credit cards, Wi-Fi networks you can share with a QR code, HomeKit and Matter setup codes, and secure notes.

**Backups you control.** Create an encrypted backup and keep it wherever you like, in iCloud Drive or anywhere else. Restore it when you set up a new iPhone.
